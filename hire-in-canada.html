Recruiting Indian Talent for Canadian Employers

TAJ HR Services supports Canadian employers with full Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) application support, coordinating with Service Canada and managing the worker's subsequent work permit application through IRCC.

Trades Commonly Placed in Canada

  • IT Workers
  • Construction Tradespeople
  • Healthcare Professionals

Work Permit Route

Most placements to Canada use an LMIA-backed work permit, with total processing of 10-12 weeks. Global Talent Stream eligible occupations, such as certain IT roles, can be expedited to as little as 2 weeks for the LMIA stage. TAJ HR Services also supports Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) applications for employers in provinces with relevant streams.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to deploy workers to Canada?

Total deployment typically takes 10-12 weeks: LMIA processing (4-6 weeks) plus work permit processing (2-4 weeks).

Do you provide LMIA support?

Yes, we provide full LMIA application support and manage the work permit application through IRCC.

Which trades does TAJ HR Services recruit for Canada?

We recruit IT workers, construction tradespeople, and healthcare professionals.
