Hiring Indian workers for Oman

Oman runs on a tighter labour framework than its neighbours. Omanisation rules decide which roles you can fill from abroad, and your Ministry of Labour clearance decides how many. We work backwards from that clearance rather than sourcing first and discovering the limit later.

What an Omani employer needs to send us

Oman adds one step that Saudi Arabia and the UAE do not: your labour clearance has to exist before anything else is worth doing. Everything below follows from it.

Ministry of Labour clearance

Confirming your establishment may employ expatriate workers in the specific job categories you want to fill, and in what numbers. This is where Omanisation limits show up in practice.

Demand letter

On company letterhead, listing job titles, number of workers per trade, monthly salary, working hours, overtime terms, accommodation, food, medical cover, transport and contract duration.

Power of attorney

Authorising TAJ HR Services to recruit and process candidates on your behalf, naming our licence number.

Specimen employment contract

The contract each worker will sign, with terms matching the demand letter exactly. Differences between the two are a common reason for eMigrate rejection.

These documents normally require attestation by the Indian Embassy in Muscat before they can be registered on eMigrate. We will tell you which attestations your specific set needs before you spend money on them.

Omanisation and emigration clearance

Two separate rule sets apply to every Oman deployment, one at the Omani end and one at the Indian end. Both have to be satisfied before a worker can fly.

Omanisation quotas

Oman reserves certain job categories and a share of positions for Omani nationals, with rules that differ by sector. This determines which roles you may fill from abroad. Your labour clearance reflects those limits, which is why we ask to see it before sourcing begins.

ECR passports need PoE clearance

Oman is a notified country under Indian emigration rules. Workers who have not passed Class 10 hold ECR passports and need Protector of Emigrants clearance through eMigrate before departure. Without it they can be stopped at the airport despite holding a valid visa.

ECNR passports do not

Class 10 pass and above is ECNR, and those workers do not need PoE clearance. The status is printed in the passport itself, so it should be checked for every candidate rather than assumed from the trade or the education claimed on a CV.

Wafid medical

Candidates must be examined at an approved GCC medical centre in India, operating under the Wafid system formerly known as GAMCA, and declared fit before visa processing. We screen early, because an unfit result after selection means restarting that position.

From clearance to departure

The sequence is fixed; only the timing changes. We give an estimated schedule once your documents are attested, rather than a headline number that ignores your sector and clearance position.

  1. Clearance and documents

    You share the labour clearance, demand letter, power of attorney and specimen contract. We check them against Indian emigration requirements and register the requirement on eMigrate.

  2. Sourcing and trade screening

    Candidates are shortlisted and screened on documents and practical trade ability. Video trade tests can be arranged where you cannot travel to India for interviews.

  3. Selection, medicals, clearance

    You select in person or by video. Selected workers complete Wafid medicals, visa processing, and PoE clearance where the passport is ECR.

Pre-departure briefing and ticketing follow, with arrival details shared with your site team before the worker flies.

Oman recruitment: common questions

What does an Omani employer need before recruiting Indian workers?

Labour clearance from the Ministry of Labour for the roles you want to fill, plus a demand letter on company letterhead, a power of attorney authorising us to recruit for you, and a specimen employment contract. These normally require attestation by the Indian Embassy in Muscat before eMigrate processing.

How does Omanisation affect hiring Indian workers?

Oman reserves certain job categories and a proportion of positions for Omani nationals, and the rules differ by sector. This decides which roles your establishment can fill with expatriate workers and how many. Your labour clearance reflects those limits, which is why we ask to see it before sourcing rather than after.

Do Indian workers going to Oman need PoE clearance?

Oman is a notified country, so Protector of Emigrants clearance applies to ECR passport holders, meaning those who have not passed Class 10. ECNR holders do not need it. Confirm the status of every worker's passport before booking flights.

Which sectors do you recruit for in Oman?

Construction and infrastructure, oil and gas support services around Sohar and Duqm, industrial and plant maintenance, transport and driving, facility management and cleaning, hospitality, and security. The roles open at any time depend on the demand letters currently in hand.

What medical examination do candidates need?

Candidates must be examined at an approved GCC medical centre in India, operating under the Wafid system formerly known as GAMCA, and declared medically fit before visa processing. We advise screening early, because an unfit result after selection means restarting that position.

Can you handle bulk requirements?

Yes. Larger requirements are mobilised in phases rather than one batch, because medicals, visa processing and clearance move at different speeds for different candidates. We plan the phasing against your site schedule so trades arrive in the order the work needs them.

Do candidates pay a fee for jobs in Oman?

Any charge to a candidate must be disclosed in advance, stay within the limits set under Indian emigration rules, and come with an official receipt. Never make a cash payment without a receipt to anyone, and verify the agency licence on emigrate.gov.in before paying anything.

Do you recruit for other Gulf countries?

Yes, we also recruit for Saudi Arabia and the UAE. European recruitment is handled separately on our other site, tajhrservices.com.
