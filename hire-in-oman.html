Hiring Indian workers for Oman

Oman runs on a tighter labour framework than its neighbours. Omanisation rules decide which roles you can fill from abroad, and your Ministry of Labour clearance decides how many. We work backwards from that clearance rather than sourcing first and discovering the limit later.

Trades we recruit for Oman

Omani demand from India concentrates around the industrial belt at Sohar and Duqm, the construction and infrastructure programme, and service contracts in Muscat and Salalah. These are the categories we work in most often.
