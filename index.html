How a requirement moves from demand letter to departure

Every deployment follows the same sequence. Timelines vary by country and visa category, so we give an estimate against your specific demand rather than a fixed promise.

  1. Demand and documents

    You share the demand letter, job order and salary terms. We check that the paperwork meets Indian emigration requirements before sourcing begins.

  2. Sourcing and trade screening

    Candidates are shortlisted from our network, then screened on documents and practical trade ability for the role you have asked for.

  3. Selection and clearance

    You interview in person or by video. Selected candidates complete medicals, visa stamping, and PoE clearance through eMigrate where the passport is ECR.

  4. Mobilisation

    Pre-departure briefing, ticketing and travel coordination, with arrival details shared with your site team before the worker flies.

PoE and ECR clearance, explained plainly

This is the step that delays Gulf deployments most often, and it is the one candidates ask about most. Saudi Arabia, Oman and the UAE are all on the list of notified countries where it applies.

ECR passports

Emigration Check Required applies to Indian passport holders who have not passed Class 10. If you hold an ECR passport and are travelling for employment to a notified country, you need Protector of Emigrants clearance through the eMigrate system before you fly. Without it you can be stopped at the airport.

ECNR passports

Emigration Check Not Required applies if you have passed Class 10 or higher, or otherwise qualify under the rules. ECNR holders do not need PoE clearance for employment travel. Check the ECR or ECNR status printed in your own passport rather than assuming.

What employers should confirm

Before booking flights, confirm each worker's passport status. Mixing ECR workers into a batch without PoE clearance is the most common cause of last-minute offloading and schedule slip.

Before you pay anyone

Check the agency's licence on emigrate.gov.in first. Any charge must be told to you in advance, stay within the legal limit, and come with a proper receipt. Never pay cash without a receipt, to anyone.

Frequently asked questions

How can I check whether TAJ HR Services holds a valid recruitment licence?

Our Ministry of External Affairs Recruiting Agent licence number is B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025. You can verify any Indian recruiting agent's licence on the Government of India eMigrate portal at emigrate.gov.in, under the registered recruiting agents list. Always check the licence before paying anything to any agency.

What is ECR and PoE clearance, and does it apply to Gulf jobs?

ECR means Emigration Check Required, and it applies to Indian passport holders who have not passed Class 10. ECR passport holders travelling for employment to the 18 notified countries, which include Saudi Arabia, Oman and the UAE, need Protector of Emigrants clearance through eMigrate before departure. Class 10 pass and above is ECNR and does not need PoE clearance.

Which trades do you recruit for in the Gulf?

Construction and civil trades, MEP and electrical, welding and fabrication, oil and gas support roles, drivers and transport, facility management and cleaning, hospitality, retail and security. The exact trades open at any time depend on the demand letters we currently hold, so contact us for the current list.

How long does deployment take from selection to departure?

It depends on the destination country, the visa category and how quickly candidate documents are completed. Gulf mobilisation typically runs several weeks from visa issuance to departure. We give an estimated timeline against each specific demand rather than a blanket guarantee.

What documents does a candidate need?

A passport with sufficient remaining validity, educational and trade certificates, experience letters where available, passport-size photographs, and a medical fitness certificate from a GAMCA or destination-approved centre. Some trades need extra certification or a licence, for example a heavy vehicle licence for drivers.

Do you also recruit for Europe?

Yes. European recruitment is handled on our separate site at tajhrservices.com. This site covers Saudi Arabia, Oman and the UAE only.
