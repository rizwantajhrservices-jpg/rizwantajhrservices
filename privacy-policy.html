Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information TAJ HR Services collects through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. It is written for both employers making hiring enquiries and candidates looking for work.

Who we are

TAJ HR Services is a recruiting agent licensed by the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India, under licence number B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025. We operate under Krewex Technologies Private Limited.

What we collect

Information you give us

When you submit the enquiry form on this website, or contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number
  • Whether you are an employer or a candidate
  • The country your enquiry relates to
  • The trades you need, or your own trade and experience
  • Anything else you choose to write in your message

Information candidates send later

If you proceed with an application, we may also receive your CV, passport details, educational and trade certificates, experience letters, photographs, and medical fitness results. We ask for these only when they are needed for a specific job application or for emigration processing.

Information collected automatically

This website does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any other tracking or profiling tools. We do not build visitor profiles.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own, and does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

Two third-party services are used on our pages:

  • Google Fonts loads the typefaces used on this site. This means Google receives your IP address when a page loads. No cookie is set for this.
  • Formspree processes our enquiry form. It only receives data at the moment you submit the form.

If we add analytics or advertising tools to this website in future, we will publish a cookie notice and ask for your consent before any non-essential tracking is loaded.

Why we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry
  • To assess whether a candidate matches an employer's requirement
  • To present shortlisted candidates to prospective employers
  • To prepare and submit documents required for visas and emigration clearance
  • To arrange medical examinations, interviews and travel
  • To keep records we are required to keep as a licensed recruiting agent

Who we share it with

Recruitment necessarily involves sharing candidate information with others. We share only what is needed, and only for the purposes above.

  • Prospective employers in Saudi Arabia, Oman and the United Arab Emirates, for candidates being considered for their vacancies
  • Government systems including the eMigrate portal and the Protector of Emigrants, where clearance is required
  • Embassies, consulates and visa processing centres for visa applications and document attestation
  • Approved medical centres for the fitness examinations required before deployment
  • Travel and ticketing providers for deployment arrangements
  • Formspree, which processes submissions from our website form

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Transfers outside India

Because we place workers with employers abroad, candidate information is transferred outside India, principally to Saudi Arabia, Oman and the United Arab Emirates. By applying for an overseas position through us, you understand that your details will be shared with employers and authorities in the destination country.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an application are kept only as long as needed to deal with them. Candidate records connected to a deployment are kept for the period required under Indian emigration rules and our licence conditions. We do not keep information longer than we need to.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to erase information we no longer need to keep
  • Withdraw your consent, though this may mean we cannot continue with an application
  • Raise a grievance about how we have handled your information

To exercise any of these, write to info@tajhrservices.in. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Our commitment on fees

Any charge to a candidate must be told to you in advance, must stay within the limits set under Indian emigration rules, and must come with an official receipt. Never make a cash payment without a receipt to anyone. You can verify our licence on the Government of India portal at emigrate.gov.in before paying anything.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not send passport scans or other sensitive documents unless we have asked you for them.

Children

Our services are for adults seeking or offering employment. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Contact and grievances

For any question about this policy, or to raise a concern, contact us at info@tajhrservices.in, call +91 96513 32002, or write to us at the address above.
