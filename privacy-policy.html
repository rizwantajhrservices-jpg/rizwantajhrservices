Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Introduction

This policy explains what personal information TAJ HR Services collects through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. It is written for both employers making hiring enquiries and candidates looking for work, and it describes what this website actually does rather than a generic list of possibilities.

Who we are

TAJ HR Services is a recruiting agent licensed by the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India, under licence number B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025. We operate under Krewex Technologies Private Limited.

  • Phone and WhatsApp: +91 96513 32002

Information we collect

Information provided by candidates

When you submit an enquiry or registration form on this website, we receive the information you type into it. Depending on the form, that is your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, trade, years of experience, preferred destination country, whether your passport is ECR or ECNR, and anything you write in the message field.

Information provided by employers and clients

Employer forms collect your name, company name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, destination country, the trades and worker numbers you need, and any details you add about the site, project or timeline.

CV and document information

This website has no file upload or CV upload function. You cannot attach a CV, passport scan or certificate through any form on this site, and we ask you not to send such documents until we specifically request them. If you later proceed with an application, we may receive your CV, passport details, educational and trade certificates, experience letters, photographs and medical fitness results by email or WhatsApp. Those are collected outside this website, and only when a specific step requires them.

Contact information

If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp using the links on this site, we receive whatever you choose to send, together with your email address or phone number. WhatsApp messages are handled within WhatsApp and are subject to its own privacy terms, which we do not control.

Information collected automatically

This website does not use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, or any other analytics, advertising or profiling tool. We do not build visitor profiles and we do not track you across other websites. As with any website, the hosting provider and the third parties named below receive technical data such as your IP address when a page loads.

How we use information

Recruitment and employment purposes

  • To assess whether a candidate matches an employer requirement
  • To present shortlisted candidates to prospective employers
  • To arrange trade tests, interviews and medical examinations
  • To prepare and submit documents required for visas and emigration clearance
  • To keep the records we are required to keep as a licensed recruiting agent

Communication

To reply to your enquiry, to tell you about a requirement that matches your profile, and to keep you informed about the progress of an application you have started. We do not send marketing newsletters from this website and we do not sell contact details to anyone.

Sharing information

Employers and clients

Recruitment necessarily involves sharing candidate information with prospective employers. We share only what is needed to assess a candidate for a specific role, and only with employers who have a genuine requirement.

Government systems and authorities

We share candidate information with the eMigrate portal and the Protector of Emigrants where emigration clearance is required, and with embassies, consulates and visa processing centres for visa applications and document attestation.

Service providers

Two third-party services are used on this website:

  • Formspree processes submissions from our enquiry forms and delivers them to our email address. It receives the form data at the moment you submit it.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on this site. Google receives your IP address when a page loads. No cookie is set for this purpose.

Outside this website, we also work with approved medical centres for fitness examinations, and with travel and ticketing providers for deployment arrangements.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. If you make a choice in our cookie preferences panel, that choice is stored in your browser's local storage so we do not have to ask again. Nothing in that record identifies you.

You can open the preferences panel at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer or the button in the corner of the screen, and change or withdraw your choice.

Analytics

We do not currently use any analytics tool on this website. The Analytics option in the cookie preferences panel exists so that if we add one in future, it will load only for visitors who have allowed it.

Third-party services and external links

Beyond Formspree and Google Fonts, this website contains no embedded third-party content. There is no YouTube embed, no Google Maps embed, no reCAPTCHA and no social media tracking script.

We link to external websites, including the Government of India eMigrate portal and our own European recruitment site at tajhrservices.com. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites we do not control, and this policy does not apply to them.

International data transfers

Because we place workers with employers abroad, candidate information is transferred outside India, principally to Saudi Arabia, Oman and the United Arab Emirates. By applying for an overseas position through us, you understand that your details will be shared with employers and authorities in the destination country. The data protection laws of those countries may differ from those of India.

Data security

This website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not send passport scans or other sensitive documents unless we have asked you for them.

Data retention

Enquiries that do not lead to an application are kept only as long as needed to deal with them. Candidate records connected to a deployment are kept for the period required under Indian emigration rules and our licence conditions. We do not keep information for longer than we need to.

Your privacy rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to erase information we no longer need to keep
  • Withdraw your consent, though this may mean we cannot continue with an application
  • Raise a grievance about how we have handled your information

To exercise any of these, write to info@tajhrservices.in. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Children's privacy

Our services are for adults seeking or offering employment. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information through this website, contact us and we will delete it.

Our commitment on fees

Any charge to a candidate must be told to you in advance, must stay within the limits set under Indian emigration rules, and must come with an official receipt. Never make a cash payment without a receipt to anyone. You can verify our licence on the Government of India portal at emigrate.gov.in before paying anything.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, or if we add a service that collects data in a new way, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Contact and grievances

For any question about this policy, or to raise a concern, contact us at info@tajhrservices.in, call +91 96513 32002, or write to us at the address above.
