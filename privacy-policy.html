Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Taj HR Services ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of candidates, employers, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website or services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal details such as name, email address, phone number, and postal address
  • Professional information including work history, qualifications, and skill certifications
  • Identity and travel documents required for visa and work permit processing
  • Company details submitted by employers via demand letters or contact forms
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser type collected automatically

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Match candidates with suitable employment opportunities
  • Process visa, work permit, and documentation requirements
  • Communicate with candidates and employers regarding recruitment status
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations under Indian and destination-country law
  • Improve our website and services

4. Data Sharing

We share candidate information only with prospective employers, relevant government authorities (such as the Ministry of External Affairs, eMigrate, and embassies), and service partners strictly for the purpose of recruitment and deployment. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at info@tajhrservices.com. We will respond to valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@tajhrservices.com or visit our office at 18-B, 1st Floor, Taimoor Nagar, Nala Road, Near Honey Money Top, New Friends Colony, New Delhi, Delhi – 110025.
