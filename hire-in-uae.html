Hiring Indian workers for the UAE

The UAE has the strictest paperwork in the Gulf about one thing in particular: the worker must sign the job offer before the entry permit is issued, and the contract signed on arrival has to match it. We build the whole process around that, so nobody lands in Dubai to find different terms waiting.

What a UAE employer needs to send us

The first thing to settle is whether you are a mainland company or a free zone company, because the two follow different authorities and different paperwork.

Mainland: MOHRE quota and work permit

Quota approval for the job categories you want to fill, followed by the work permit application through the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation.

Free zone: authority approval

Free zone companies process through their own zone authority rather than MOHRE. Tell us which zone you are in at the start so we prepare the correct document set.

Demand letter

On company letterhead, listing job titles, number of workers per trade, monthly salary, working hours, overtime terms, accommodation, food, medical cover, transport and contract duration.

Power of attorney and specimen contract

Authorising TAJ HR Services to recruit on your behalf, with the contract each worker will sign. Terms must match the demand letter exactly, or eMigrate registration is likely to be rejected.

These documents normally require attestation by the Indian Mission in the UAE before they can be registered on eMigrate. We will confirm which attestations your specific set needs before you spend money on them.

Offer letters, clearance and pay protection

Three safeguards sit between a UAE selection and a worker starting on site. Each exists because of a problem that used to be common, and each is worth understanding on both sides.

The job offer is signed first

Under UAE labour rules the standard job offer must be signed by the worker before the work permit and entry permit are issued, and the contract signed later must match it. This is what stops terms changing on arrival. We make sure candidates read and understand the offer instead of signing blind.

ECR passports need PoE clearance

The UAE is a notified country under Indian emigration rules. Workers who have not passed Class 10 hold ECR passports and need Protector of Emigrants clearance through eMigrate before departure. Without it they can be stopped at the airport despite holding a valid entry permit.

Medical checks, before and after

Where the employer or visa category requires it, candidates are screened in India at an approved GCC centre under the Wafid system, formerly GAMCA. After arrival, workers complete a medical fitness test in the UAE as part of the residence visa and Emirates ID process.

Wage Protection System

Mainland employers pay salaries through the Wage Protection System, which records payments electronically. Candidates should expect their salary through this route, at the amount written in the signed offer and not a different figure quoted verbally by anyone.

From quota to arrival

The sequence is fixed; only the timing changes. We give an estimated schedule once your documents are attested, rather than a headline number that ignores whether you are mainland or free zone.

  1. Approvals and documents

    You share the quota or free zone approval, demand letter, power of attorney and specimen contract. We check them against Indian emigration requirements and register the requirement on eMigrate.

  2. Sourcing, screening and offer

    Candidates are shortlisted and screened on documents and practical trade ability. Selected workers read and sign the standard job offer before the entry permit is applied for.

  3. Permits, medicals, clearance

    Entry permit issued, medicals completed, and PoE clearance obtained through eMigrate where the passport is ECR.

Pre-departure briefing and ticketing follow, with arrival details shared with your team before the worker flies. Residence visa and Emirates ID formalities are completed in the UAE after arrival.

UAE recruitment: common questions

What does a UAE employer need before recruiting Indian workers?

For mainland companies, quota approval and a work permit application through MOHRE. Alongside that we need a demand letter on company letterhead, a power of attorney authorising us to recruit for you, and a specimen employment contract. Free zone companies process through their own zone authority instead, so tell us which applies at the start.

Does the worker have to sign the job offer before travelling?

Yes. Under UAE labour rules the standard job offer must be signed by the worker before the work permit and entry permit are issued, and the contract signed later must match it. This protects the worker against terms being changed on arrival. We make sure candidates read and understand the offer before signing.

Do Indian workers going to the UAE need PoE clearance?

The UAE is a notified country, so Protector of Emigrants clearance applies to ECR passport holders, meaning those who have not passed Class 10. ECNR holders do not need it. Confirm the status of every worker's passport before booking flights.

Which trades do you recruit for the UAE?

MEP and air conditioning trades, electrical and plumbing, carpentry and finishing, warehouse and logistics, drivers and delivery riders, security, housekeeping and cleaning, and hospitality and retail roles. The trades open at any time depend on the demand letters currently in hand.

What medical checks do candidates need?

Where the employer or visa category requires a pre-departure examination, candidates are screened at an approved GCC medical centre in India under the Wafid system, formerly GAMCA. After arrival, workers also complete a medical fitness test in the UAE as part of the residence visa and Emirates ID process.

How are salaries paid to workers in the UAE?

Mainland employers pay through the Wage Protection System, which records salary payments electronically. Candidates should expect their salary through this route, at the amount written in the signed job offer rather than a different figure quoted verbally.

Do candidates pay a fee for jobs in the UAE?

Any charge to a candidate must be disclosed in advance, stay within the limits set under Indian emigration rules, and come with an official receipt. Never make a cash payment without a receipt to anyone, and verify the agency licence on emigrate.gov.in before paying anything.

Do you recruit for other Gulf countries?

Yes, we also recruit for Saudi Arabia and Oman. European recruitment is handled separately on our other site, tajhrservices.com.
