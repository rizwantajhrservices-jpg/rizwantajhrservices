Who we are

TAJ HR Services is an overseas recruitment agency based in New Friends Colony, New Delhi, operating under Krewex Technologies Private Limited.

We hold a Recruiting Agent licence issued by the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India. That licence is what allows us to recruit Indian workers for employment abroad, and it is the first thing any employer or candidate should check before dealing with any agency, including us.

What this site covers

This site is for Gulf recruitment only: Saudi Arabia, Oman and the United Arab Emirates. We concentrate on these three markets so our team stays current on each one's visa route, attestation requirements and labour authority process rather than spreading thinly across many countries.

Our European and Canadian recruitment work is handled separately at tajhrservices.com.

The work we actually do

Recruitment is mostly documentation. Between an employer signing a demand letter and a worker landing on site, there are attestations, eMigrate registration, medicals, visa stamping and emigration clearance, each with its own way of going wrong. Our job is to keep that sequence moving and to tell you honestly where it stands.

For employers

We check your demand documents against Indian emigration requirements before sourcing starts, so problems surface early rather than at the clearance stage.

For candidates

We screen on documents and practical trade ability, explain the terms in the offer before anything is signed, and tell you in advance about any charge, with a receipt.

Trade screening

Candidates are tested on the trade they are being put forward for. Employers can interview in person or by video, and trade tests can be recorded where travel is not practical.

Emigration clearance

Saudi Arabia, Oman and the UAE are all notified countries. We handle Protector of Emigrants clearance through eMigrate for candidates holding ECR passports.

What we do not promise

Whether a candidate is selected is the employer's decision. Whether a visa or emigration clearance is granted is the decision of the relevant government authority. We do not guarantee selection, visa issuance, clearance or a fixed timeline, and we would treat any agency that does with caution. Where we give estimated timelines, they are estimates against a specific requirement.

Our position on candidate fees

Any charge to a candidate is told to you in advance, stays within the limits set under Indian emigration rules, and comes with an official receipt. We do not take cash without a receipt. If anyone asks you for money in our name outside these terms, contact us before paying.
