About Taj HR Services

Connecting Talent With Global Opportunities

Taj HR Services is focused on international recruitment and manpower solutions, helping employers connect with skilled, semi-skilled and professional candidates from India.

Who We Are

A recruitment partner for employers and candidates

Taj HR Services works in the field of recruitment and manpower coordination, with a focus on international employment opportunities.

We aim to understand the actual requirements of employers and connect those requirements with suitable candidates based on skills, experience and job specifications.

For candidates, our goal is to provide clear information about available opportunities and guide them through the applicable recruitment process.

✓ Employer-focused recruitment
✓ Skilled & semi-skilled manpower
✓ Professional & technical recruitment
✓ Candidate screening & coordination
✓ Interview & selection coordination
✓ International deployment support

Our Approach

Every recruitment requirement is different. We therefore focus on understanding the position before sourcing candidates.

  • Understand employer requirements
  • Identify relevant candidate profiles
  • Screen candidates according to requirements
  • Coordinate interviews and assessments
  • Support documentation processes
  • Coordinate applicable deployment activities
