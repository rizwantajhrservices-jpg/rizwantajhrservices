Our Recruitment Process

From Requirement to Deployment

A structured process helps us understand your requirement and coordinate candidate recruitment.

1

Requirement

We understand your job roles, quantity, skills, experience, salary and destination.

2

Sourcing

Suitable candidates are sourced through our recruitment network and database.

3

Screening

Profiles are reviewed according to your specified skills and experience.

4

Assessment

Trade testing or technical assessment can be arranged where applicable.

5

Interview

Shortlisted candidates are coordinated for employer interviews and final selection.

6

Documentation

Selected candidates complete applicable employment and recruitment documentation.

7

Processing

Applicable permit, travel and pre-departure processes are coordinated.

8

Deployment

Candidates are prepared and coordinated for departure and arrival.
