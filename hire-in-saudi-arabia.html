What a Saudi employer needs to send us

Indian emigration rules require specific attested documents before any recruitment can legally begin. Getting these right at the start is what separates a four-week mobilisation from a four-month one.

Demand letter

On company letterhead, listing job titles, number of workers per trade, monthly salary, working hours, overtime terms, accommodation, food, medical cover, transport and contract duration.

Power of attorney

Authorising TAJ HR Services to recruit and process candidates on your behalf, naming our licence number.

Specimen employment contract

The contract each worker will sign, with terms matching the demand letter exactly. Mismatches between the two are a common reason for eMigrate rejection.

Visa authorisation

Your block visa or work visa authorisation reference from the Saudi labour authorities, needed before Enjaz submission and stamping.

These documents normally require attestation by the Indian Mission in Saudi Arabia before they can be registered on eMigrate. We will tell you exactly which attestations your specific set needs before you spend money on them.

Emigration clearance for Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is one of the notified countries where Protector of Emigrants clearance applies. This is where most last-minute airport problems come from, so it is worth understanding before you book anything.

ECR passports need PoE clearance

Emigration Check Required applies to Indian passport holders who have not passed Class 10. For employment travel to Saudi Arabia, these workers need clearance through the eMigrate system before departure. Without it, they can be stopped at the airport even with a valid visa.

ECNR passports do not

Emigration Check Not Required applies to Class 10 pass and above, and to some other categories. These workers do not need PoE clearance. The status is printed in the passport itself, so it should be checked rather than assumed.

GAMCA or Wafid medical

Every worker must be examined at an approved medical centre and declared fit before visa stamping. We advise screening candidates early, because a candidate found unfit after selection means restarting that slot from scratch.

Enjaz and visa stamping

Once the visa authorisation is issued and documents are in order, applications go through the Saudi mission's processing channel for stamping. Passport validity and photograph specifications are strict, and rejections here are usually avoidable.

From demand letter to departure

The sequence is fixed; only the timing changes. We share an estimated schedule once your documents are attested, rather than a headline number that ignores your specific visa category.

  1. Documents and registration

    You send the demand letter, power of attorney and specimen contract. We check them against Indian emigration requirements and register the requirement on eMigrate.

  2. Sourcing and trade screening

    Candidates are shortlisted and screened on documents and practical trade ability. Video trade tests can be arranged where you cannot travel for interviews.

  3. Selection, medicals, clearance

    You select in person or by video. Selected workers complete GAMCA medicals, visa stamping, and PoE clearance where the passport is ECR.

Pre-departure briefing and ticketing follow, with arrival details shared with your site team before the worker flies.

Saudi Arabia recruitment: common questions

What does a Saudi employer need to send before recruitment can start?

A demand letter on company letterhead with job titles, worker numbers, salary and benefits; a power of attorney authorising us to recruit for you; a specimen employment contract; and the visa authorisation reference once issued. These normally need attestation by the Indian Mission in Saudi Arabia before eMigrate processing.

Do Indian workers going to Saudi Arabia need PoE clearance?

Saudi Arabia is a notified country, so Protector of Emigrants clearance applies to ECR passport holders, meaning those who have not passed Class 10. ECNR holders do not need it. Confirm the status of every worker's passport before booking flights.

What is the GAMCA medical and who needs it?

GAMCA, now operating as Wafid, is the approved medical examination system for workers travelling to GCC countries. Every candidate must be examined at an approved centre and declared fit before visa stamping. Candidates declared unfit cannot proceed, which is why we screen early rather than after selection.

How long does Saudi deployment take?

It depends on how quickly the demand documents are attested, how fast candidates complete medicals, and current processing speed at the Saudi mission and on eMigrate. We give an estimate against your specific requirement once documents are in hand, not a fixed guarantee.

Can you handle bulk requirements?

Yes. Larger requirements are usually mobilised in phases rather than a single batch, because medicals, stamping and clearance all move at different speeds for different candidates. We plan the phasing with your site schedule so that trades arrive in the order your work needs them.

Do candidates pay a fee for Saudi jobs?

Any charge to a candidate must be disclosed in advance, stay within the limits set under Indian emigration rules, and come with an official receipt. Never make a cash payment without a receipt to anyone, and verify the agency's licence on emigrate.gov.in before paying anything.

Do you recruit for other Gulf countries?

Yes, we also recruit for Oman and the UAE. European recruitment is handled separately on our other site, tajhrservices.com.
