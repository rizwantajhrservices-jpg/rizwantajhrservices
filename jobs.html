How to Apply for Overseas Jobs

If you are interested in an opportunity, keep your updated CV/resume and relevant documents ready.

✓ Updated CV / Resume
✓ Passport details when requested
✓ Education certificates where applicable
✓ Experience certificates where applicable
✓ Relevant trade certificates
✓ Passport-size photograph when required
✓ Valid contact number

Only apply for positions that match your skills, experience and qualifications.

Before You Apply

Please carefully check the complete job details and selection conditions.

  • Job title
  • Country / location
  • Salary package
  • Working hours
  • Accommodation
  • Food / allowance
  • Transportation
  • Contract duration
  • Selection process
  • Applicable documentation
