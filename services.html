Our Process

How International Recruitment Works

A structured recruitment process designed around employer requirements.

1

Employer Requirement

We understand job roles, quantity, skills, experience, salary and deployment requirements.

2

Candidate Sourcing

Our recruitment team sources suitable candidates from our network and database.

3

Screening & Testing

Candidates are screened and assessed according to the position and employer requirements.

4

Interview & Selection

Selected profiles are presented for employer interviews and final selection.

5

Documentation

Selected candidates complete the required documentation and applicable formalities.

6

Permit Processing

Work permit and related process coordination is completed according to the destination.

7

Pre-Departure

Candidates receive applicable orientation and pre-departure preparation.

8

Deployment

Travel, arrival and post-deployment coordination complete the recruitment journey.
